# Orden del Magén - Product Roadmap

## Business Model

- **Café de la Doncella LLC**: Property acquisition and ownership company
- **Orden del Magén**: Community engagement and property management platform

The platform enables:
1. Strategic property acquisition (identifying, evaluating, acquiring properties)
2. Portfolio management (tracking owned properties and performance)
3. Community building (creating engaged communities around each property)
4. Maintenance coordination (care and upkeep)
5. Financial reporting (portfolio performance and ROI)

## Technology Stack

- **Frontend**: React + Vite
- **Backend**: Node.js + Express
- **Database**: PostgreSQL
- **Auth**: JWT + roles
- **File Storage**: AWS S3 or local
