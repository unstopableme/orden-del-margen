# Whitepaper concept: Café de la Doncella

> **Status: narrative concept only.** This document is not an offer to sell
> securities, tokens, memberships, or financial products. No token sale,
> wallet, custody, payment, airdrop, NFT, or blockchain functionality is
> implemented in the MVP.

## 1. The premise

Café de la Doncella is a playful fraternity of dreamers: a circle of people
bound by stories, coffee, donuts, knowledge, and quests that help their
communities. The order's first practical rule is simple: membership is for
adults age 18 and older, subject to the laws and requirements of the
member's jurisdiction.

The current product expresses the legend through member-owned profiles,
community topics, knowledge games, planet-care and creature-protection
quests, and non-monetary appreciation gifts.

## 2. Narrative token concept

The story describes a fictional Doncella Token, **DULCÍNEA**, as a scarce
membership badge with a planned supply of 20,000. The story's proposed
allocation is:

| Allocation | Share | Narrative purpose |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Public offering | 50% | Future member access concept |
| Treasury | 25% | Café upkeep and future quests |
| Founders | 15% | Time-locked story allocation |
| Community rewards | 10% | Quests, art, and community deeds |

The previously discussed fundraising and per-token price are intentionally
not product requirements. Any real-world token, sale, redemption, transfer,
airdrop, reward, or membership instrument would require independent legal,
tax, securities, consumer-protection, age-verification, jurisdiction, and
security review before it could be designed or announced as a product.

## 3. What the MVP does instead

- Membership requires an 18+ age check.
- Members own and edit their profile, status, and topics.
- Knowledge-based PvP scores and community quest points are non-monetary.
- Coffee gifts are appreciation messages, not purchases or stored value.
- The property information surface is informational and creates no ownership
  or investment rights.

## 4. The legend continues

Future chapters may use the Café, Don Quixote, Sancho, and the Doncella as
fictional framing for quests, events, art, and educational content. Narrative
language must remain clearly separate from legal promises, financial returns,
ownership claims, or an invitation to invest.
