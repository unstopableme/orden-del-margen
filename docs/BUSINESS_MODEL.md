# Business Model and Launch Proposals

**Status:** Documentation-only proposal; not adopted and not authorized for implementation
**Scope:** User-provided business facts, launch sequencing, commercial proposals, and unresolved decisions
**Last updated:** 2026-10-05

## 1. Document boundary

This document records user-provided facts and future business proposals for
review. It does not establish legal ownership, a revenue entitlement, a
payment contract, a sales policy, a refund policy, a token allocation, or a
go-live commitment. No statement here authorizes code, payment processing,
financial reporting, property acquisition, NFT issuance, DAO operation, or
database state changes.

## 2. User-provided organizational facts

The following are recorded as facts provided by the user and have not been
independently verified in this repository:

- Café de la Doncella LLC is formed in New Mexico.
- Café de la Doncella LLC is owned by Juan Verduzco.

These statements are not a substitute for legal, corporate, tax, or ownership
records. No repository artifact should be treated as evidence of either fact.

## 3. Starting position and launch order

- The venture starts with zero available funds.
- The digital book launches first.
- The RPG follows closely after the book launch.

Launch timing, funding sources, operating budgets, staffing, production costs,
and the meaning of “closely” remain unresolved.

## 4. Confirmed business-design chapter access decisions

The following are confirmed business-design decisions for documentation
purposes. They are not implementation authorization, payment authorization,
runtime access rules, or reward commitments:

- A BTC purchase immediately unlocks all 12 book chapters and the
  corresponding 12 RPG chapters.
- Chapters 1–3 are free.
- Completing Kingdom Tower Levels 1–9 unlocks Chapters 4–12 respectively:
  KTL 1 unlocks Chapter 4, KTL 2 unlocks Chapter 5, and so on through KTL 9
  unlocking Chapter 12.

These decisions authorize no implementation and do not change Kingdom Tower
requirements, Tower progression rules, participant verification, or reward
eligibility. They do not establish a price, payment contract, entitlement to
company revenue, property ownership, token value, or a production access
service.

Purchases grant content access only. Every player faces the same Kingdom Tower
progression requirements and the same reward-eligibility requirements;
purchase status must not lower, bypass, or otherwise change those rules.

For documentation purposes, the confirmed BTC rule describes a full
12-chapter book/RPG bundle: one purchase unlocks all 12 book chapters and the
corresponding 12 RPG chapters. The bundle's price remains unresolved. The
document also does not define wallet custody, payment confirmation,
chargebacks, regional availability, tax treatment, or access revocation.

Kingdom Tower completion requirements beyond the confirmed chapter mapping and
chapter completion semantics remain unresolved. The content-access rule does
not change progression or reward eligibility. Progression text must not be
interpreted as an entitlement to company revenue, property ownership, or token
value.

## 5. Future revenue and property direction

Future revenue streams may include:

- coffee distribution;
- property rentals and leasing; and
- book, RPG, membership, or other commercial products subject to separate
  approval.

Target acquisitions may include homes, farms, and commercial properties in the
United States and Mexico. Any acquisition, lease, rental, financing, title,
or operating plan requires separate legal, financial, tax, compliance, and
operational review.

Land preservation and eco-friendly modernization are intended to guide the
business. These are strategic principles, not property-specific commitments,
environmental certifications, investment promises, or guaranteed outcomes.

## 6. Confirmed surplus-allocation basis and split

The confirmed business-design basis for any future surplus allocation is
available net surplus after expenses, applicable taxes, and required reserves.
The allocation must not be calculated from gross revenue.

After that basis is calculated, the confirmed allocation is:

- 70% for property acquisitions; and
- 30% for book/RPG development and owner distributions.

Reserve targets, reserve categories, and release conditions remain unresolved.
This allocation is a business-design decision only: it is not an ownership
right, revenue entitlement, dividend, token allocation, or payment promise. No
holder, player, purchaser, member, token owner, or participant receives a
claim from this document.

## 7. Future digital ownership and voting proposals

NFT-based ownership qualification remains a future proposal with undefined
legal rights. Digital assets, game land, NFTs, or internal balances must not be
described as shares, securities, title, rent, income entitlements, redemption
rights, or real-property ownership without separate approval.

Quantity-based DAO voting also remains a future proposal. The voting asset,
quantity calculation, quorum, delegation, proposal scope, execution authority,
legal effect, and relationship to company governance are undefined. No DAO,
smart contract, public pool, or voting process is authorized by this document.

## 8. Explicit unresolved decisions

The following decisions must remain open:

- commercial delivery terms for the full book/RPG bundle;
- required reserve categories, reserve targets, and release conditions;
- launch costs, funding sources, operating runway, and production budget;
- exact Kingdom Tower completion requirements for Chapters 4–12;
- whether access can be transferred, revoked, suspended, or shared;
- applicable sales terms, refund terms, cancellation terms, chargebacks, taxes,
  consumer disclosures, and regional restrictions;
- BTC payment confirmation, custody, volatility, accounting, and compliance;

- conservation and eco-modernization standards and how outcomes are measured;
- any NFT ownership qualification or DAO voting design and their legal rights.

## 9. Relationship to existing project documents

This proposal is compatible with the existing high-level vision that Café de
la Doncella may use profits from books, café sales, paid memberships, and the
game to fund property purchases. Existing documents also keep digital-game
assets separate from real-property ownership and treat NFTs, blockchain
contracts, public pools, and player voting as future proposals.

The following points require reconciliation before adoption:

- Existing economy and decision documents describe a proposed paid Season 1
  chapter at an `$8.99` equivalent paid in BTC. That wording is not adopted as
  the commercial definition here; the confirmed business-design rule is a full
  12-chapter book/RPG bundle, with purchase price unresolved.
- Existing roadmap material describes a free starter experience and a proposed
  paid Season 1 chapter. Those references require reconciliation with the
  confirmed full-bundle rule and do not authorize checkout or access logic.
- Existing documents use Kingdom Tower Level 3 as a proposed reward-eligibility
  gate. This document records the confirmed business-design decision for KTL
  1–9 chapter unlocks, which must not alter reward eligibility.
- Existing Community and architecture documents prohibit payment processing,
  custody, securities, legal title, and NFT/blockchain runtime behavior in the
  current MVP. Those boundaries remain unchanged.

## 10. Implementation boundary

This document authorizes no implementation. Do not create payment routes,
wallet custody, checkout logic, chapter gates, revenue ledgers, surplus
allocation jobs, acquisition workflows, NFT contracts, DAO contracts, or
property records from this proposal alone.

All facts, proposals, percentages, chapter mappings, and launch statements
remain subject to separate business, legal, financial, product, security,
privacy, and operational approval.
